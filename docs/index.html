Who we are

Xtratech Global Solutions Limited is a private technology company incorporated in Nigeria in March 2019. We provide technology, fintech infrastructure, enterprise software and digital transformation solutions for businesses, financial institutions, government institutions and organisations.

We help organisations build technology, connect systems, process payments, automate operations, collect revenue, digitise services and transform their businesses.
