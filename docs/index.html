Who we are

Xtratech Global Solutions Limited is a private technology company incorporated in Nigeria in March 2019. We design, build and operate fintech infrastructure, digital financial platforms and business technology products.

We solve real problems for banks, microfinance institutions, fintech companies, enterprises and ambitious organisations that need reliable technology.

Headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (RC 1565165).
